Kitchen Fitting

Kitchens

A new kitchen, from stripping out the old one to fitting the last piece of trim. We also take on the building work that goes with it: moving or removing a wall, blocking up a doorway, forming a new opening, so the room ends up with the layout you want rather than the one it came with.

Old kitchen stripped out
Units, worktops and appliances fitted
Walls, doorways and openings altered
Plastering, flooring and finishing

What the work involves

We start by taking the existing kitchen out and getting back to the bare walls and floor, so anything hidden behind the old units can be looked at before the new ones go in. If the layout is changing, the structural and first-fix work happens at this stage: openings formed, walls made good, services moved to where the new run of units will sit.

Fitting and finishing

Units are set out and levelled against the room rather than assumed square, because very few Norwich houses are. Worktops, sink, appliances, splashbacks, flooring, skirting and trim follow. We finish the plastering and decorating around the new kitchen so the room is complete rather than handed back needing another trade.
