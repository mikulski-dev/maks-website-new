About Norwich Builders

Over 20 Years of Building Experience

At Norwich Builders, we bring over 20 years of experience in the building industry to every project we undertake.

Now proudly serving homeowners across Norwich and Norfolk, we specialise in kitchens, bathrooms, extensions, renovations, conversions and a wide range of general building work.

Our experience over the years has given us the knowledge, skills and attention to detail needed to take on projects of all sizes. Whether you’re looking to update a single room, create more space with an extension or completely transform your property, we approach every project with the same commitment to quality.

Quality Workmanship & Personal Service

We believe that choosing a builder is about more than just finding someone to carry out the work. It’s about finding a team you can trust in your home.

As a small, independent building company, we offer a friendly and personal service. We work closely with our customers to understand their requirements, provide practical advice and keep the process as straightforward as possible from start to finish.

We take pride in our workmanship and pay close attention to the details that make a real difference to the finished result.
